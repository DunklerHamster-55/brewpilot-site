Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Exclusively on your iPhone. BrewPilot has no account, no server and no cloud requirement. If you delete the app, the data is gone — but it is part of your regular iPhone backup.

Why don't I see "Ask your coach" or the AI recognition?

These features use Apple Intelligence directly on the device and are available on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and newer) and have it enabled in iOS Settings. On other devices, import still works — with automatic detection.

The import from a shop page doesn't find every detail.

BrewPilot reads what the page provides in machine-readable form, and that differs from shop to shop. Take a quick look at the pre-filled fields and add what is missing; a bean is never saved without your confirmation.

BrewPilot doesn't appear in Safari's share menu.

The first time: in the share menu, swipe all the way to the right → "More" → "Edit" → enable BrewPilot. After that it stays in place.

How do I add the widgets to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold the Home Screen → "+" at the top left → search for "BrewPilot" → pick a widget and place it. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → "Customize".

I received a .brewpilot file — what now?

That's a bean from a friend! Just tap the file (in AirDrop, WhatsApp, Mail or the Files app) — BrewPilot opens and offers the bean for your library. If you haven't installed BrewPilot yet, get it from the App Store first.
